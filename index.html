The two collisions

C1 Stick (perfectly inelastic): two matching faces merge into one face of the next tier, but only if the crash releases enough kinetic energy (Easy 4 J, Normal 9 J, Hard 16 J). Slower hits bounce or stack. Momentum is kept, kinetic energy is lost.

KE lost = ½ · μ · v², μ = m₁m₂ ÷ (m₁ + m₂), v = closing speed

So a Tier 1 pair (μ = 0.5 kg) needs a fast hit, while a Tier 6 pair (μ = 16 kg) merges with a gentle bump.

v′ = (m₁v₁ + m₂v₂) ÷ (m₁ + m₂)

C2 Bounce (elastic): different faces bounce apart. Momentum and kinetic energy are both kept.

v₁′ = [(m₁−m₂)v₁ + 2m₂v₂] ÷ (m₁+m₂)   v₂′ = [(m₂−m₁)v₂ + 2m₁v₁] ÷ (m₁+m₂)

Scoring

Each merge scores |Δp| × 10 points (rounded up), where Δp = m₂(v′ − v₂) is the momentum given to the target face. Merges within 1 second of each other multiply their points (×2, ×3, up to ×5). Missions give bonus points.

Masses: Tier 1 to 10 = 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64, 128, 256, 512 kg. Gravity g = 10 m/s². Launch speed up to 12 m/s.

Rocks (12 kg) never merge. A rock breaks when a hit gives it a change in momentum Δp of at least 20 (Easy), 30 (Normal) or 45 (Hard) kg·m/s, scoring Δp × 10.

The box: faces are fired from a slingshot on a 2.55 m cliff into an open box (walls 5.0 m high, 4.5 m on Hard). Walls and the ground take away half the speed of a hit. A face that falls out of the box ends the game; a shot that misses the box just rolls away.

Box shake: the box jolts, giving every face in it the same change in velocity, Δv of about 5 m/s up plus a sideways part toward the middle (up to 4 m/s, so faces are not thrown out). The impulse on each face is J = mΔv, so heavy faces take a much bigger impulse than light ones. Faces fly at different angles and crash into each other, which can merge stuck small faces. You start with 1 shake, earn 1 every 5 merges, and can hold up to 3. Merges caused by a shake don't count toward the next one. The shake itself is an outside force (like the walls), so it is not logged, but the collisions it causes are.

Not logged: hits on walls and the floor (outside forces), and faces resting in the pile slower than 0.6 m/s.
